# Hey, I'm Aaryaman.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Vishwakarma Institute of Technology, Pune. I build things at the intersection of AI, computer vision, automation and real-world systems — and I like taking them further than a notebook or a demo.

A lot of my work is about making the whole thing hold together: the data, the model, the backend, the interface, and the decisions the system is actually allowed to make. Recently, that has meant maritime intelligence, agentic financial systems and medical imaging.

## Selected projects

- **NeuroTrace** — Brain MRI analysis combining 3D U-Net segmentation, interactive 2D/3D visualisation, quantitative analysis and a research assistant powered by Gemma 4.
- **[ResolveX](https://github.com/aaryamanmishra/ResolveX)** — An evidence-first payment intelligence and fraud investigation platform, with agentic workflows, graph investigation and human approval for sensitive actions.
- **[Maritime Operations AI](https://github.com/aaryamanmishra/maritime-operations-ai)** — A maritime situational-awareness platform combining satellite SAR vessel detection, AIS intelligence and weather-aware voyage evaluation.
- **[Ghost Internet AI](https://github.com/aaryamanmishra/ghost-internet-ai)** — A research tool for surfacing abandoned ideas, with academic discovery and patent-related enrichment.
- **[TigerGraph Agentic Fraud Investigation](https://github.com/aaryamanmishra/tigergraph-agentic-fraud-investigation)** — Graph-native fraud investigations that gather evidence, reason about uncertainty and keep policy decisions deterministic.
- **[GPR Image Enhancement](https://github.com/aaryamanmishra/GPR-Image-Enhancement)** — Deep learning and image-processing work on ground-penetrating radar imagery.
